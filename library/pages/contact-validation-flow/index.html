Step 1 of 4 — Connect your conduits

You're about to create three conduits pointing at the same Google Sheet, each with a different access level — the exact permission model every conduit you build from here on uses.

  1. POST Write-only. Accepts new entries; nothing can be read back through it.
  2. GETPATCH Read + update. Lists entries and edits them.
  3. GET Read-only. Lists entries; any write is rejected outright, not just hidden.
